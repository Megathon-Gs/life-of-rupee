# Level 10 — What the policy leaves out

Location ID: `insurance-desk` · Interaction: DocumentInspector, then OfferComparator

Draft narrative; financial claims and language await sourced educator review.

## Hook

> Ishan: “This future-adult practice case has an unexpected bill.”
> Ishan: “Let's check what the policy covers before counting on it.”

## Situation

Future-adult Mina inspects a fictional policy, then compares two covers at Ishan's desk. The document states coverage, exclusions, deductible and cap; the practice incident is vehicle damage from a stated collision, covered under these fictional terms. Emergency savings help plan for costs outside the cover.

Mina need not buy insurance or own a vehicle to demonstrate understanding. The case explores preparedness rather than a sales decision.

## Trap or misconception

“Having insurance means every repair is fully paid.” Coverage has boundaries; routine maintenance differs from an insured event. A cheaper premium alone does not show how well a policy fits the stated need.

## Consequence

For a covered incident, the preview separates the eligible amount from the deductible, cap and remaining cost under sample terms. For routine servicing excluded by this sample policy, the policy does not pay the bill; emergency savings matter. Lack of insurance never causes a breakdown, and breakdown is not punishment for a choice.

## Transfer twist

Use two changed incidents or policy terms; at least one incident is excluded and must be met from emergency savings or a revised plan. Explain which clause applies before comparing covers.

## Scripted realistic mistake path

Mina marks routine vehicle servicing as covered. Ishan says, “Find the line that describes this kind of work.” The player reads the exclusion, corrects the answer and includes the remaining expense in the plan. The different-incident transfer is answered correctly.

Explain: inspect the incident and actual terms; emergency savings complement insurance rather than replace every form of it. This ordinary corrected path targets 2 stars, with 3 available on clean replay and the first attempt preserved. Inspection supports Ishan's later recognition, not a promise of future coverage.
