# Level 2 — Festival money, the right route

Location ID: `bank` · Interaction: DocumentInspector lesson, then FormFiller decision and transfer

As written in the existing level and English/Telugu locales. Story review remains pending.

## Hook

> Mina: I want to save my festival money, but I'm still a minor.
> Teller: Account rules can differ for minors. A guardian may need to help.
> Mina: Let's check the documents and choose the right account route.

## Situation

The existing DocumentInspector lesson contrasts a sample passport with a school library card. The player then travels to `bank`. FormFiller asks for Mina's account route and identity option: the configured answers are `minor` and `passport`. The alternatives are an adult route and assuming Aadhaar is the only option.

Both locales retain Mina and the unnamed teller; neither names the teller Leela. Every document is a fictional sample. These are constrained practice choices, not a complete real application or a guarantee that presenting a passport opens an account.

## Trap or misconception

“Every account follows the adult route,” or “Aadhaar is the only possible identity document.” The existing lesson distinguishes minor-account safeguards and applicable document options. A guardian **may** need to help; requirements depend on the bank and applicable rules. Rejecting the library card concerns the offered OVD choices, not every possible use of school documents.

## Consequence

The existing outcome says “Fictional account opened.” Its explanation says the route matches the young account holder, real banks determine exact requirements, and real personal or financial details must never be entered. Completion sets `bank.accountOpened = 'true'`, supporting the teller's recognition on return.

## Transfer twist

The existing pool has two genuinely different cases: Tara, 16, with guardian present and a sample passport; Arun, 21, opening his first account without Aadhaar, with a sample voter ID. Correct routes are minor/passport and adult/voter ID respectively. Both English and Telugu describe those same cases.

## Scripted realistic mistake path

Select `adult` for Mina's `accountRoute`, correct it to `minor`, choose `passport`, then answer the selected transfer correctly. This single route error uses the existing `bank.wrong_document` category and gives **2 stars**. A clean retry gives **3**; best becomes 3 while the first result remains 2. Identity errors use `kyc.wrong_document`; adding them is not the fixed 2-star path.

The review explains matching age and safeguards to the route rather than shaming Mina.
