# Level 5 — A message that wants a hurry

Location ID: `phone-shop` · Interaction: MessageTriage

Draft narrative; financial claims and language await sourced educator review.

## Hook

> Ravi: “This message wants an answer before you've read it.”
> Ravi: “Let's check what it's asking you to do.”

## Situation

At the same phone shop, Mina sorts fictional messages and flags warning signs. A prize message claims to be official, pressures her to act now and asks for information or payment. Another provides a routine notice whose context can be independently verified.

The player inspects urgency, impersonation, links, OTP/PIN requests and implausible prizes. No live link opens; reporting routes belong in official-source-reviewed level content.

## Trap or misconception

“It sounds official, so it must be safe,” or “acting quickly protects me.” A claimed identity is not proof. Pressure replaces careful checking with an immediate unsafe action.

## Consequence

The simulated preview shows that the request would disclose a secret or send money, not secure a prize. Mina stops, verifies independently through the taught official route and uses the appropriate reporting action. Feedback explains the risk without blaming her for finding it convincing.

## Transfer twist

Use two changed messages: a different impersonation with the same urgency cue, and a prize or request with different wording. Identify the demand, not a copied phrase. The urgency cue reappears in L9's conditional easy-loan ad.

## Scripted realistic mistake path

For the ordinary 2-star script, Mina misclassifies a harmless routine notice as suspicious. She has not clicked, disclosed or paid. Ravi asks, “Can we verify this notice independently?” She checks the provided trusted source and context without following the message's link, corrects its classification, then identifies the actual pressure message and answers the transfer safely. Explain: inspect the request and verify its source independently. Absence of a money or secret request does not prove a message safe.

OTP/PIN disclosure or paying fraud is critical: unresolved earns 0; corrected caps the attempt at 1. Clean replay can earn 3 without erasing the first result. Completing the lesson supports Ravi's recognition and the L9 callback; it never means a student suffered real fraud.
