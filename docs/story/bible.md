# Paisa Street — story bible

Draft for NGO educator and language review. These pages supply story direction, not final financial claims or legal advice. Level authors source and verify facts, numbers and assumptions before release.

## One reason to keep going

**Mina** is a fictional older school student learning to make her first allowance last and save her festival money for a reading lamp for her study space. She initially mistakes the biggest number or quickest route for the best choice. Her growing habit is simple: “What does this leave me with?” She learns to ask that about money, time, safety and obligations. The player helps Mina practise; nobody supplies their own identity or finances.

L1–7 stay close to student decisions. L8–10 explicitly use **fictional future-adult Mina**, with simulated income, borrowing and insurance. L11 explores goals without telling a student what to buy. L12 brings the ideas together in one labelled future-adult practice month. This is a look ahead, not a claim that a minor has taken employment, a vehicle loan or a policy. No purchase or loan is required to succeed.

## The same street

The urban neighborhood contains Mina's home and the bank: places to begin, revise a plan and ask questions. The phone shop makes digital payment visible through an ordinary counter interaction. The high street connects market, workplace and showroom: receipts, earnings and commitments become things Mina can inspect rather than intimidating vocabulary. The rural-connected stretch shares maintained streets, smartphones, businesses and everyday infrastructure; the insurance desk and learning centre fit naturally among community services. Later placements are narrative direction, not new district assignments in code.

People travel both ways. No district is a reward for escaping another, and no skyline signals greater worth. Use readable details—shade, awnings, a bus stop, a study corner—rather than poverty scenery, tourist imagery, accents or stereotypes. Retain the contemporary, warm, teen-appropriate tone across districts.

## Remembered choices, small consequences

- `bank.accountOpened = 'true'`: Leela recognises progress on return. Recognition means “we have practised this,” never automatic real-world eligibility.
- `savings.goalMet = 'true'`: the established home upgrade acknowledges Mina's modest goal. A better-looking room is not a better person.
- `fraud.seenWarningSigns = 'true'`: Ravi recognises Mina; L9's conditional easy-loan ad repeats L5's urgency cue. A fresh profile gets no claim of remembering it. P1-09 fixes conditional inclusion at attempt start, including after resume.
- `loan.activeInstallment`: a recorded fictional adult repayment commitment appears as recurring pressure at home and in the **L6 return visit's** budget. Initial L6 precedes L9 and has no invented loan. This optional revisit is not a backwards prerequisite, new level or required unlock; declining borrowing creates no installment.
- `insurance.policyInspected = 'true'`: Ishan recognises the inspection; a later case still checks its actual incident and exclusions. No insurance choice causes a breakdown.
- `curriculum.completed = 'true'`: after L12, the neutral **Financial Futures Centre** opens when the existing capstone feature is enabled. With it off, the exterior stays locked. The P1 flag-gated concept is a preview, not L12 completion. Further learning remains optional; no sponsor names, promotions or automatic external trips belong in these stories.

No vehicle traversal change, economy, random setbacks, new quests or extra branching systems are required.

## Tone, privacy and feedback

Short hooks lead to action. Let a receipt, date or changed budget explain the consequence. NPCs say what to check, not “you should have known.” Wants are legitimate; revision and postponement are competent decisions. Never shame a learner, equate wealth with success or deduct stars for reading speed, language or accessibility needs.

Use fictional names and obviously synthetic labels such as `XXXX-DEMO` and `SAMPLE ONLY`. Any future handle must visibly be a demo placeholder with no real provider. No live-looking identifiers, links, contact numbers, credentials or user-entered personal data. Generic concepts such as UPI, Aadhaar, PAN and FD are permitted; real people, companies, banks, insurers and products are not.

Ordinary corrected mistake paths use one noncritical error, correction and a correct transfer, with no additional errors or hints; they target 2 stars under their briefs; a clean replay can earn 3. Best stars improve while the first attempt stays recorded privately. Do not overwrite it. An unresolved critical PIN/OTP disclosure or payment to fraud earns 0; correction caps that attempt at 1, never 2. Scripts here are authoring targets, not scoring code. Replay does not promise to clear an existing obligation or undo remembered choices.
