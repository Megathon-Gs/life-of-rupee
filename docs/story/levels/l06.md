# Level 6 — The total at the counter

Location ID: `market` · Interaction: ReceiptInspector, then BudgetAllocator

Draft narrative; financial claims and language await sourced educator review.

## Hook

> Nila: “Here's your receipt. Does the total fit your plan?”
> Nila: “The shelf tag is only where we start.”

## Situation

Mina checks a fictional receipt at Nila's billing counter, then revises a short budget. Price, discount, tax and final total appear under labelled assumptions. The lesson connects his allowance plan to what actually leaves it.

Initial L6 assumes no later loan. On a return after L9, an existing fictional installment appears in the budget if recorded. This optional return is not a prerequisite or a change to curriculum order.

## Trap or misconception

“The tag price is exactly what I'll pay.” This misses the stated discount and tax treatment. On return, another trap is treating money committed to repayment as freely spendable.

## Consequence

The final total leaves less than Mina reserved using the tag alone. Nila helps check the lines and adjust spending. In the adult-simulation return case, an installment also reduces room for shopping and savings; the bill itself does not change because he borrowed.

## Transfer twist

Use two different receipts, including one where the discount is applied before tax. Ask for the payable total and a budget that covers it. The installment return retains its future-adult label and invents no loan for a player who declined one.

## Scripted realistic mistake path

Mina selects the tag amount as the total. Nila points to the discount, tax and payable lines: “Follow these through to the last line.” The player selects the correct total and reallocates the budget to cover essentials and any existing commitment. The new receipt is answered correctly.

Explain: use the final bill and reserve commitments before allocating the remainder. Exact arithmetic comes from verified level content. This ordinary corrected path targets 2 stars; clean replay targets 3. Best improves while the first attempt remains recorded.
