# Level 7 — The same basket, later

Location ID: `market` · Interaction: TimeSlider

Draft narrative; financial claims and language await sourced educator review.

## Hook

> Nila: “Same shopping list. Different year's prices.”
> Nila: “What will the same money buy this time?”

## Situation

Back at Nila's counter, Mina compares an ordinary basket of goods across illustrative years. The time slider changes price tags using a labelled inflation assumption. He compares the basket's cost with money held or saved over the same period.

This is a demonstration, not a prediction of actual town prices or a random setback. Familiar shopping makes the difference between a number and purchasing power visible.

## Trap or misconception

“My balance hasn't fallen, so its value hasn't changed,” or “any savings increase keeps me ahead.” The money amount and what it buys are different questions. Growth below the assumed price increase may leave the basket less affordable.

## Consequence

At the later time point, unchanged money covers less of the same basket under the assumption. Even a growing balance can fall short if prices rise faster. Revising the goal amount or timeline explains the trade-off; no character is punished for inflation.

## Transfer twist

Two new baskets use different rates and time spans. Ask what the money can afford and how purchasing power changed, rather than which nominal balance is largest. Include savings growth below the assumed price increase.

## Scripted realistic mistake path

Mina answers “unchanged” because the money figure is unchanged. Nila asks, “Can it still pay for this same list?” The player moves the slider, compares the later basket cost and corrects the purchasing-power answer. The changed-basket transfer is answered correctly.
