# Level 12 — One month that holds together

Location ID: `home` · Interaction: ScenarioChain

Draft narrative; financial claims and language await sourced educator review.

## Hook

> Mina: “This future-adult practice month puts the choices together.”
> Mina: “Let me check what each decision leaves for the next.”

## Situation

At home, Mina works through one fictional adult month. ScenarioChain links a budget, bank or UPI check, loan or insurance choice and savings decision. Existing interactions, including BudgetAllocator, supply the short tasks; this is not a new quest or economy.

Income and commitments belong to the stated case. Relevant remembered choices add context without inventing a repayment for someone who declined borrowing. All money and identities are synthetic.

## Trap or misconception

“Each choice is sensible by itself, so the whole plan must work.” Spending the apparent remainder before reserving a commitment makes a later step fail. Safe payment alone does not show that budget, protection and savings fit together.

## Consequence

The month shows what remains after each decision. An optional expense crowds out a commitment or emergency reserve, so Mina revises the plan. Success demonstrates reasoning rather than ownership or wealth.

After curriculum completion, the Financial Futures Centre opens when its existing feature is enabled. Further learning follows the home capstone; the centre is not L12's location or a product shop.

## Transfer twist

Two different-month cases change income or commitments; one inserts fraud midway. Check the request safely and carry the remaining budget into the next decision. No real credentials or random disaster are involved.

## Scripted realistic mistake path

Mina allocates the remainder to an optional café outing while overlooking the practice month's rent commitment. The next budget check reveals the shortfall. She reserves rent, reduces the outing spend, verifies the bank/payment step and completes protection and emergency-savings choices. The new-month transfer is answered correctly.

Explain: a plan must cover connected obligations, not merely each isolated screen. This ordinary corrected path targets 2 stars; clean replay targets 3 with the first result preserved. Unresolved critical disclosure or payment to fraud earns 0; correction caps that attempt at 1. Educator review remains pending.
